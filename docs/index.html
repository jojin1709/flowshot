Features

Everything you need to
document workflows

From recording to export, FlowShot handles it all automatically.

Smart Blur

Automatically detects and blurs sensitive data: emails, phone numbers, credit cards, SSNs, IP addresses. Toggle each category independently.

AI Descriptions

Bring your own API key and get human-readable step descriptions. 15-30x cheaper than vision models — uses DOM context, not screenshots.

Guide Me Replay

Replay any guide live on a real page. Highlights the next element, tracks progress, and advances automatically as you interact.

Voice Narration

Talk through the workflow while recording. FlowShot transcribes your narration and matches it to each step automatically.

Guide Editor

Fix guides after recording. Crop, annotate, redact screenshots, rewrite steps with AI, add headings, reorder, and version history.

Multi-Format Export

Export as Video (mp4), PDF, DOCX, HTML, or Markdown. All generated client-side — nothing touches a server.

How It Works

Three steps to
perfect documentation

01

Record

Click the FlowShot icon and start recording. Perform your workflow normally — every click, keystroke, and navigation is captured automatically.

02

Capture

Each action becomes a step with an annotated screenshot. Smart blur protects sensitive data. AI generates clear descriptions.

03

Share

Export your guide as Video, PDF, DOCX, HTML, or Markdown. Replay it live on any page. Share with your team.
